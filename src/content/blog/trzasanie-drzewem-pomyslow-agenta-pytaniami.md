## Part 1 of the agent-native harness series

It isn't the biggest thing an agent gives you — but it's a large, nearly free win: instead of assigning a task, shake the tree of possibilities with a question. One question knocks loose a fruit I'd never have seen myself, and the agent — holding the full session's context — answers with something concrete, not a generality. Cost: one sentence. Return: a branch that would never have grown otherwise.
